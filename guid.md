# Complete Glow-Up Program

Male, 26, 175 cm, 66 kg, Morocco

Main target:
- Phase 1: reach `72 kg` while staying relatively lean
- Phase 2: push to `75 kg` while staying under `20%` body fat

This plan focuses only on realistic, non-surgical improvements with the highest return:
1. Clear skin
2. Better haircut and grooming
3. Muscle gain and posture
4. Better clothes fit
5. Strong hygiene and presence

## 1. Face And Skin

### What usually improves male facial attractiveness
- Clear, even skin tone
- Less puffiness and bloating
- Lower facial fat if currently puffy
- Stronger neck and posture
- Clean brows and facial hair
- Good haircut with volume and shape
- Healthy lips, teeth, and under-eye area

Your biggest wins without surgery:
- Consistent sunscreen
- Simple skincare routine
- Better sleep and hydration
- Less sodium-heavy junk food
- Resistance training plus gradual weight gain, not dirty bulking
- Better posture and neck position

### Morning skincare
1. Cleanser
2. Niacinamide serum or simple moisturizer
3. Moisturizer
4. Sunscreen SPF 50 every day

#### Morning routine example
1. Wash face with a gentle cleanser for 20 to 30 seconds
2. Apply `niacinamide 4% to 10%` if you get oiliness, redness, or post-acne marks
3. Apply lightweight moisturizer
4. Apply `2-finger amount` of sunscreen to face and neck

### Night skincare
1. Cleanser
2. Treatment depending on skin type
3. Moisturizer

#### Night routine by skin type

##### If acne-prone or oily
- Cleanser
- `Salicylic acid 2%` serum 3 nights per week
- Moisturizer

##### If dull or uneven skin tone
- Cleanser
- `Niacinamide` most nights
- `Adapalene` or `retinol` 2 nights per week if tolerated
- Moisturizer

##### If dry or sensitive
- Gentle cleanser
- Moisturizer on damp skin
- Add `panthenol`, `ceramides`, or `hyaluronic acid`

### Best ingredients to prioritize
- `Niacinamide`: controls oil, redness, marks
- `Salicylic acid`: unclogs pores, helps acne and blackheads
- `Adapalene` or low-strength `retinol`: improves acne, texture, long-term skin quality
- `Ceramides`: repair skin barrier
- `Panthenol`: soothing
- `Hyaluronic acid`: hydration
- `Azelaic acid`: good for acne marks and redness
- `Sunscreen SPF 50`: most important anti-aging product

Do not buy everything at once.

Start with:
1. Cleanser
2. Moisturizer
3. Sunscreen
4. Then add one treatment only

### Weekly skin routine
- `1 to 2x/week`: salicylic acid if you get clogged pores
- `1x/week`: clay mask only if you are very oily
- `0 to 1x/week`: gentle exfoliation, not scrubs
- `Daily`: lip balm
- `Daily`: sunscreen reapply if outdoors more than 2 to 3 hours

### Rules that matter
- Never use harsh scrubs
- Never pick acne
- Do not start 3 active ingredients at once
- Patch test new products
- If skin burns for days, stop and simplify

## 2. Face Improvement Without Surgery

### How to improve jawline appearance
- Keep body fat under control during bulk
- Fix neck posture: chin slightly tucked, head not pushed forward
- Train upper back and rear delts
- Chew normally, do not overdo jaw gadgets
- Keep beard lines clean if you grow facial hair
- Reduce puffiness and water retention

### Reduce bloating and face puffiness
- Sleep `7.5 to 9 hours`
- Drink `2.5 to 3 liters` water daily
- Stop very salty late-night meals most days
- Reduce alcohol
- Get enough potassium from bananas, potatoes, oranges, tomatoes
- Walk daily
- Avoid dirty bulking with pizza, fries, pastries, soda

### Beard advice by face shape

#### If face is round
- Keep sides shorter
- More length on chin
- Clear cheek line
- Avoid very wide full beard

#### If face is long
- Keep more fullness on sides
- Avoid very long goatee-only styles

#### If face is square
- Short boxed beard works well
- Keep it neat, not too bulky

#### If patchy
- Clean stubble `1 to 3 mm` usually looks better than forcing a full beard

### How to choose your beard
- Weak chin: short boxed beard or stubble with more definition near jaw
- Good jaw already: clean stubble often looks best
- Patchy cheeks: low stubble

### Eyebrow grooming
- Do not make them thin
- Only remove obvious stray hairs:
  - middle unibrow area
  - very low stray hairs under brow
  - random long hairs
- Brush them upward after shower
- Trim only hairs sticking out too much

### Haircut recommendations
Ask your barber for:
- low taper fade
- mid taper with textured top
- short sides, more volume on top
- clean neckline and temple area

Best options for most men:
- `Textured crop`
- `Short quiff`
- `Side part with taper`
- `Curly top with taper` if you have curls

Avoid:
- overly flat top
- too much bulk on the sides if your face is round
- bad hairline edges that look artificial

Hair priorities:
- haircut every `2 to 4 weeks`
- use a small amount of matte clay or paste
- keep hair clean but do not shampoo aggressively every day if scalp gets dry

## 3. Body Transformation

### Goal
You are `66 kg`. A realistic path is:
- `72 kg` first in 4 to 6 months
- then `75 kg` if the waist stays controlled

Best rate of gain:
- `0.25 to 0.5 kg per week`

### Calories and macros

## 5. Hygiene And Presence

### Daily hygiene checklist
- Shower
- Clean face morning and night
- Brush teeth `2x/day`
- Floss `1x/day`
- Tongue clean
- Deodorant
- Clean underwear and socks
- Trim nails
- Moisturize lips
- Keep beard and neckline clean

### Smell good consistently
- Use unscented or mild deodorant daily
- Use one signature fragrance
- Spray fragrance on:
  - neck sides
  - chest
  - maybe one spray on clothes
- Do not overspray in heat

### Good fragrance directions for men
- Fresh everyday: citrus, aquatic, clean woody
- Date night: amber, vanilla, spicy woody
- Summer in Morocco: fresh and light

### Posture and body language
- Chest up, ribs down
- Chin slightly tucked
- Shoulders back and relaxed
- Walk slower
- Do not keep hands nervously in pockets all the time
- Speak a bit slower
- Make eye contact without staring

### Confidence habits
- Lift weights consistently
- Keep promises to yourself
- Limit doom scrolling
- Improve one visible area every 2 weeks
- Take progress photos monthly
- Stop comparing yourself every day
